# CityPark Parking Facilities — General Information

## About CityPark

CityPark operates three modern, secure parking facilities across the city, serving commuters, shoppers, visitors, and residents since 2008. Our mission is to provide safe, affordable, and convenient parking that keeps the city moving. Each facility is professionally staffed and monitored around the clock.

Whether you need a quick hourly stop or a multi-day stay, CityPark has the right option for you. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees, a straightforward online and in-person booking process, and facilities equipped for modern needs including EV charging and accessible bays.

## Our Three Facilities

### CityPark Central (Downtown)
Our flagship indoor facility located at the heart of the business district. Five levels of covered parking with premium security. Ideal for shoppers, office workers, and event attendees. Capacity: 350 spaces.

### CityPark Eastside (Business District)
A modern outdoor facility near the east business corridor and tech hub. Affordable rates, well-lit perimeter, and close to major transit links. Capacity: 220 spaces.

### CityPark Airport (Airport Connector)
24-hour operation adjacent to the City Transit Airport Connector Terminal. Perfect for travelers requiring short-term or extended stays. Shuttle service to the terminal every 15 minutes. Capacity: 480 spaces.

## Core Values

- **Transparency** — Our pricing is posted on-site and online. What you see is what you pay.
- **Safety** — Every facility is covered by CCTV and staffed 24/7 (CityPark Airport) or during open hours.
- **Accessibility** — Dedicated spaces for drivers with disabilities at no extra charge in all facilities.
- **Sustainability** — EV charging is available at all three sites; we are actively expanding capacity.
