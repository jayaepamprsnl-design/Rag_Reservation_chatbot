# CityPark — Payment Methods

## Accepted Payment Methods

CityPark accepts a wide range of payment options so you can pay in the way that is most convenient for you.

### Credit and Debit Cards
Visa, Mastercard, and American Express are accepted at all facilities — at the exit kiosk, the on-site pay stations, and online. Cards are processed via encrypted terminals that comply with PCI DSS Level 1.

### Contactless and Mobile Payments
Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay are accepted at all physical terminals. Hold your device or watch near the contactless reader until you see the green confirmation light.

### Cash
Cash is accepted at the entry and exit kiosks at CityPark Central and CityPark Eastside. **CityPark Airport is cashless** — please use a card or digital wallet there. Exact change is not required; kiosks dispense up to $50 in change.

### CityPark Pre-paid Account
Frequent users can open a CityPark account and load credit in advance. Account holders receive:
- **5% discount** on all bookings.
- One-tap payment in the mobile app.
- Monthly itemised statements for expense claims.

Sign up at citypark.example.com/account or ask staff at any facility.

### Invoice / Corporate Billing
Businesses parking 10+ vehicles per month are eligible for monthly invoice billing. Contact our corporate team at corporate@citypark.example.com for an account application.

## Receipts and Invoices

- Digital receipts are emailed automatically for all app and online transactions.
- Printed receipts are available at exit kiosks on request.
- Duplicate receipts can be requested within 90 days via info@citypark.example.com.

## Overstay Charges

If you stay beyond your booked end time, overstay is charged at the standard hourly rate for that facility, rounded up to the nearest 30 minutes. Overstay charges are billed to the card on file for pre-booked sessions, or must be settled at the exit kiosk for walk-in sessions.
