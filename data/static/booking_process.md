# CityPark — Booking Process

## How to Make a Reservation

CityPark offers three booking channels: the AI chatbot on our website, the mobile app, and the on-site kiosk. All channels confirm availability in real time.

### Via the CityPark AI Chatbot (Recommended)

1. Open the chat on our website or mobile app.
2. Tell the assistant you would like to make a reservation. Example: *"I'd like to book a parking space at CityPark Central on Friday."*
3. The assistant will ask for:
   - Your **full name** (as it appears on your ID).
   - Your **vehicle licence plate number** (letters, digits, and hyphens — e.g. ABC-1234).
   - Your preferred **facility** (Central, Eastside, or Airport).
   - Your **arrival date and time** (must be at least 1 hour in the future).
   - Your **departure date and time** (must be after arrival).
4. Review the summary the assistant presents, including the total estimated cost.
5. Type **"confirm"** or **"yes"** to finalise the booking.
6. You will receive a **booking reference number** (e.g. CPK-20261030-0047). Save this — you will need it at the entrance barrier.

### Via the Mobile App

Download the CityPark app (iOS and Android). Create a free account, go to **Book a Space**, choose your facility, date/time range, and confirm payment details. Your confirmation appears instantly in the app and is emailed to you.

### At the On-Site Kiosk

Walk-in customers can use the kiosk at each facility entrance. Space availability is shown in real time. Pay by card or cash; the kiosk prints a ticket valid for the session you select.

## Modifying a Reservation

- Reservations can be modified up to **2 hours before the start time** at no charge, subject to availability.
- To modify, use the chatbot ("I want to change my reservation CPK-20261030-0047"), the app, or call customer service.

## Arrival

- Pull up to the barrier and either scan your QR code confirmation or enter your booking reference on the keypad.
- The barrier will raise automatically. Proceed to any available bay in your assigned zone.
- If the barrier does not respond, press the intercom button for assistance.

## Departure

- Drive to the exit barrier before your pre-booked end time. The barrier reads your plate automatically.
- Any overstay is charged at the standard hourly rate and billed to the card on file (app/online bookings) or payable at the exit kiosk (walk-in bookings).
