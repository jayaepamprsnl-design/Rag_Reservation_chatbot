# CityPark — Cancellation and Refund Policy

## Overview

We understand that plans change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both customers and the facility, which must reserve space in advance.

## Free Cancellation Window

Reservations may be cancelled **free of charge** up to **2 hours before the booked start time**. You will receive a full refund to your original payment method within 3–5 business days.

## Late Cancellations

If you cancel between 2 hours and 30 minutes before your start time, a **50% cancellation fee** applies. The remaining 50% is refunded within 3–5 business days.

## No-Show Policy

If you do not arrive within 30 minutes of your booked start time and have not contacted us, the reservation is marked as a **no-show**. No refund is issued for no-shows.

*Exception:* If you are delayed due to documented circumstances (accident, medical emergency, severe weather), contact us within 24 hours with supporting evidence. We will review such cases individually and may issue a full or partial refund.

## Same-Day Bookings

Reservations made less than 2 hours before the start time are **non-refundable** from the moment of booking, as we cannot re-sell the space on short notice.

## How to Cancel

1. **Via the chatbot:** Tell the assistant "I want to cancel reservation CPK-YYYYMMDD-XXXX." The assistant will confirm the cancellation fee (if any) and ask for your confirmation before proceeding.
2. **Via the mobile app:** Go to **My Bookings**, select the reservation, and tap **Cancel Booking**.
3. **Via customer service:** Call +1 (555) 200-7000 or email cancellations@citypark.example.com with your booking reference.

## Refund Timelines

| Payment Method       | Refund Timeline        |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| Credit / Debit Card  | 3–5 business days      |
| Apple / Google Pay   | 3–5 business days      |
| CityPark Account     | Instant credit         |
| Cash (kiosk)         | Cheque posted within 7 days |

## Modifications vs. Cancellations

Changing the date, time, or facility of a reservation is treated as a **modification**, not a cancellation, provided the original booking is not within 30 minutes of starting. Modifications are free up to 2 hours before the original start time. If a modification is not possible due to availability, you will be offered a cancellation under the standard policy.

## Force Majeure

In cases where CityPark must close a facility due to circumstances beyond our control (severe weather, power outage, government order), all affected reservations will be cancelled automatically and **fully refunded**, regardless of the normal cancellation window.
