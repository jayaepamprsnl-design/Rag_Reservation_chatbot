# CityPark — Security and Safety

## Our Commitment to Security

The safety of our customers, their vehicles, and their belongings is our highest priority. CityPark invests continuously in physical and electronic security infrastructure across all three facilities.

## CCTV Surveillance

All areas of all facilities — including approach roads, entry/exit barriers, driving aisles, pedestrian walkways, stairwells, and elevators — are covered by high-definition IP cameras operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Footage is retained for 30 days and can be released to law enforcement upon request. Camera locations are clearly signed as required by law.

## On-Site Staff

- **CityPark Central and Eastside:** Staffed during operating hours (06:00–22:00 daily). A security guard patrols the facility every 30 minutes.
- **CityPark Airport:** Staffed and patrolled 24 hours a day due to round-the-clock operations.

All staff carry radios and are trained in first aid, fire evacuation, and conflict de-escalation.

## Lighting

All outdoor areas are illuminated to a minimum of 50 lux throughout the hours of darkness. Internal covered areas maintain full lighting at all times. Emergency lighting activates automatically within 3 seconds of a power failure.

## Access Control

- Entry and exit barriers operate via licence plate recognition (LPR) and booking QR codes.
- Pedestrian access after hours requires a registered account PIN.
- Perimeter fencing at CityPark Eastside is 2.4 m high with anti-climb topping.

## Emergency Procedures

**In case of fire:**
Activate the nearest red break-glass alarm point. Evacuate via the green exit signs. Do not use elevators. Assembly point is marked on each level.

**Emergency phone points** (blue pillars with yellow handsets) are located every 50 m and connect directly to the on-site control room. Press the button to speak; no dialling required.

**First-aid kits** are located at every staffed kiosk.

## Tips for Safe Parking

- Lock your vehicle and take your keys.
- Do not leave valuables visible on seats — use the boot or in-facility lockers.
- Be aware of your surroundings when walking to and from your vehicle, especially at night.
- Park in well-lit areas close to exits when possible.
- If you witness suspicious activity, report it to staff immediately or call our security line: +1 (555) 200-7911.

## Incident Reporting

To report vehicle damage, theft, or any other incident, speak to on-site staff or email security@citypark.example.com with your booking reference, date/time, and a description of the incident. We will acknowledge your report within 4 business hours.
