# CityPark — Frequently Asked Questions

## Reservations

**Q: Can I make a same-day reservation?**
A: Yes, as long as the start time is at least 1 hour in the future. Walk-in spaces are also usually available, but reserving in advance guarantees your spot and can be cheaper.

**Q: What if I arrive early or late?**
A: Your reserved bay is held for up to 30 minutes after your booked start time. Arriving early is usually fine — use the kiosk to check in and the system will start your session. If you expect to arrive more than 30 minutes late, modify your booking via the app or chatbot.

**Q: Can I extend my booking while I am already parked?**
A: Yes. Use the CityPark app, the chatbot, or the in-facility pay stations to extend your session, subject to availability. You cannot extend after your end time has already passed.

**Q: Can I book for someone else?**
A: Yes. Provide the driver's name and their vehicle's licence plate when making the reservation. The booking confirmation can be shared with the driver.

## Payment

**Q: When am I charged?**
A: For online and app bookings, your card is authorised at booking and charged on exit (adjusted for any overstay). Walk-in customers pay at the exit kiosk.

**Q: What if I cannot pay at the exit barrier?**
A: Press the intercom button at the exit barrier. A staff member will assist and arrange an alternative payment method. You will not be trapped — we can always raise the barrier manually.

**Q: Are there any hidden fees?**
A: No. The price shown at booking is the price you pay, unless you overstay your booked period. Overstay is charged at the standard hourly rate.

## Vehicles

**Q: Can I park a van or large SUV?**
A: Standard bays accommodate vehicles up to 5.0 m × 2.2 m × 2.0 m. Oversized or high-top vans must use our designated areas — call ahead to confirm availability: +1 (555) 200-7000.

**Q: Is motorcycle parking available?**
A: Yes, at CityPark Central (Zone C, 15 bays) and CityPark Eastside (8 dedicated motorcycle bays). Motorcycles pay the standard car rate as bays are full-size.

**Q: Can I leave my car overnight?**
A: Yes. Register your vehicle at the exit kiosk before the facility closes (not applicable at CityPark Airport, which is 24/7). Overnight rates apply from midnight.

## Technical Issues

**Q: My QR code is not scanning at the barrier.**
A: Enter your booking reference manually on the keypad (format: CPK-YYYYMMDD-XXXX). If that fails, press the intercom for staff assistance.

**Q: The app is not showing my booking.**
A: Pull down to refresh the bookings screen. Check that you are signed in with the correct account. If the problem persists, call +1 (555) 200-7000 with your booking reference.

## Other

**Q: Is there a loyalty programme?**
A: Yes. Every dollar spent earns 1 CityPark Point. 200 points = $10 credit. Points expire after 12 months of inactivity.

**Q: Can I use the facility if I am not a registered user?**
A: Absolutely. Walk-in customers are welcome. You do not need an account to park — just take a ticket at the barrier and pay on exit.
