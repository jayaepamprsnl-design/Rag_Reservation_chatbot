# CityPark — Location and Directions

## CityPark Central (Downtown)

**Address:** 123 Central Avenue, Downtown, City DC 10001
**GPS:** 40.7128° N, 74.0060° W

### Getting Here

**By Subway/Metro:**
Exit at Central Station (Lines 2, 4, 6). Take the Main Street exit and walk two blocks north on Central Avenue. The entrance is on the left, marked by green CityPark signage.

**By Bus:**
Routes 12, 14, and 27 stop directly outside the entrance on Central Avenue. Route 9 stops one block away on Park Street.

**By Car (from Northbound I-95):**
Take Exit 14A toward Downtown. Follow signs for Central Avenue. Turn left on 1st Street, then right onto Central Avenue. The facility entrance is 200 m on the right.

**By Car (from Southbound I-95):**
Take Exit 14 toward City Center. Merge onto Route 9 South. Turn right at Central Avenue. Entrance is on the right after the traffic lights.

---

## CityPark Eastside (Business District)

**Address:** 45 Eastside Boulevard, East Business District, City DC 10024
**GPS:** 40.7205° N, 73.9857° W

### Getting Here

**By Subway/Metro:**
Exit at Eastside Tech Station (Line 7). The facility is a 5-minute walk east along Eastside Boulevard.

**By Bus:**
Routes 31 and 55 stop at Eastside Boulevard & Commerce Street, one block from the entrance.

**By Car:**
From downtown, take Commerce Street east to the junction with Eastside Boulevard. Turn right; the facility entrance is 100 m ahead on the left. Overhead signs are visible from the junction.

---

## CityPark Airport (Airport Connector)

**Address:** 1 Airport Connector Road, Transit Zone, City DC 10088
**GPS:** 40.6413° N, 73.7781° W

### Getting Here

**By Shuttle:**
Free shuttle buses run every 15 minutes between the Airport Connector Terminal (Arrivals Level, Bay 7) and CityPark Airport. Journey time: approximately 8 minutes.

**By Car:**
Follow Airport Connector Road signs from the main airport ring road. CityPark Airport is signed from the terminal roundabout — follow the blue "Long Stay Parking" signs. Do not follow signs for Short Stay Terminal Car Park (a separate facility).

**Tip:** Allow extra time during peak travel periods (Friday afternoons, Sunday evenings, and public holidays).
