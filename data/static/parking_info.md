# CityPark Parking Facility — General Information

## About Us
CityPark is a modern, secure parking facility located in the heart of downtown.
We offer indoor and outdoor parking for cars, motorcycles, and small vans.
Our facility is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Location
Address: 123 Central Avenue, Downtown City, DC 10001
GPS Coordinates: 40.7128° N, 74.0060° W
Nearest Metro: Central Station (2-minute walk)
Nearest Bus Stops: Routes 12, 14, 27 stop outside the entrance.

## Parking Zones

### Zone A — Indoor (Premium)
- Fully covered, climate-controlled
- 24/7 CCTV and staffed security
- EV charging points available (pre-booking required)
- Rate: $5.00 / hour

### Zone B — Outdoor (Standard)
- Open-air, fenced perimeter
- Well-lit overnight
- Rate: $3.00 / hour

### Zone C — Motorcycle Bay
- Dedicated motorcycle bays with lockable bollards
- Rate: $1.50 / hour

## Working Hours
Open every day: 06:00 – 22:00
Last entry accepted at 21:30.
Vehicles left overnight must be registered at the kiosk.

## Booking & Reservation Process
1. Tell the assistant you'd like to make a reservation.
2. Provide your full name, car license plate number, desired date, and start/end times.
3. Review the confirmation summary and reply "yes" to confirm.
4. On arrival, check in at the entrance kiosk with your name or plate number.
5. Payment is collected on exit (cash, card, or app).

## Facilities & Amenities
- CCTV surveillance 24/7
- Covered walkways to nearby building exits
- Restrooms on Level 1 (Zone A)
- Electric vehicle charging points in Zone A (pre-booking required)
- Accessible (disabled) spaces available in all zones — no surcharge
- Bicycle storage locker room near Zone B entrance

## Cancellation Policy
Reservations may be cancelled up to 2 hours before the start time at no charge.
Late cancellations incur a 50% fee.
