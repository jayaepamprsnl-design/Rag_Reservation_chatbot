# CityPark — Accessibility

## Our Commitment to Inclusive Parking

CityPark is committed to providing a welcoming and barrier-free experience for all customers, including those with disabilities, mobility challenges, or other access needs. All facilities comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and local accessibility regulations.

## Accessible Parking Spaces

| Facility             | Accessible Bays | Location                          |
|----------------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------|
| CityPark Central     | 18 bays         | Ground Level, near elevator lobby |
| CityPark Eastside    | 10 bays         | Front row, nearest to pedestrian exit |
| CityPark Airport     | 24 bays         | Level 1, near shuttle bus stop    |

- All accessible bays are wider than standard (3.8 m × 5.5 m) to accommodate ramps and transfer space.
- **No surcharge** for accessible bays. Standard parking rates apply.
- Blue Badge / disability parking permit required to use these bays. Permits must be displayed visibly. Staff may check validity during busy periods.

## Physical Access Features

- **Step-free access** throughout all facilities. No steps between parking areas and pedestrian exits.
- **Lifts/Elevators** at CityPark Central and CityPark Airport (ground to all levels). Minimum door width 900 mm. Braille and audio floor announcements fitted.
- **Dropped kerbs** at all pedestrian crossings within facilities.
- **Tactile paving** guides at pedestrian entry and exit points.
- **Wide-aisle routes** marked for wheelchairs, mobility scooters, and pushchairs throughout.

## Restroom Facilities

- Accessible restrooms (grab rails, turning space ≥ 1500 mm) are located on the ground floor of CityPark Central near the exit lobby.
- Accessible portable sanitation units at CityPark Eastside (reviewed and replaced quarterly).
- Fully accessible restrooms at CityPark Airport on Level 1 near the shuttle stop.

## Assistance

On-site staff are trained to assist customers with disabilities. If you require help parking, operating the barrier, or navigating the facility:

- **Press the blue intercom button** on any emergency pillar.
- **Call ahead:** +1 (555) 200-7050 (accessibility helpline, Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00).
- **Inform the chatbot** at the time of booking ("I will need assistance on arrival") and we will arrange for a staff member to meet you at the entrance.

## Hearing and Visual Impairments

- Exit kiosks are fitted with audio prompts and large-print displays.
- Induction loops are installed at all staffed kiosk counters.
- Signage throughout uses high-contrast text (black on yellow) in a minimum 24-point font.

## Feedback

We continuously work to improve accessibility. If you experience any barriers or have suggestions, please email accessibility@citypark.example.com or ask to speak with the Duty Manager on site.
