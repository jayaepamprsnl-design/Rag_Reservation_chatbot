# CityPark — Parking Rules and Regulations

Please read these rules carefully. Compliance ensures a safe and orderly experience for all customers.

## General Rules

1. **Valid reservation or ticket required.** All vehicles must have either a confirmed reservation or a valid entry ticket obtained at the barrier on arrival.
2. **Display confirmation.** Keep your booking confirmation number accessible at all times. Staff may request it during busy periods.
3. **One space per reservation.** Each booking covers a single standard vehicle. Oversized vehicles must contact us in advance.
4. **Do not block aisles.** Vehicles left in driving lanes, in front of exits, or across bay markings will be wheel-clamped and may be towed at the owner's expense.
5. **Maximum vehicle dimensions.** Standard bays accommodate vehicles up to 5.0 m long × 2.2 m wide × 2.0 m tall. Taller vehicles (vans, SUVs with roof racks) must use designated high-clearance areas.

## Speed and Driving

- Maximum speed inside all facilities: **10 mph (16 km/h)**.
- Follow one-way traffic markings at all times.
- Give way to pedestrians at all crossings.
- Do not reverse into bays unless signage indicates it is required.

## Prohibited Activities

- Smoking and vaping inside any covered structure.
- Running repairs, oil changes, or mechanical work on site.
- Charging personal e-scooters or e-bikes except at designated charging points.
- Leaving animals unattended in vehicles for extended periods (30+ minutes).
- Commercial vehicle idling for more than 5 minutes.

## Overnight Parking

- Vehicles staying after closing time must be pre-registered at the exit kiosk before the facility closes.
- Unregistered vehicles found after closing may be treated as abandoned and reported to city authorities.
- CityPark Airport is open 24/7 and has no overnight registration requirement.

## Enforcement

- CCTV operates continuously. Violations are logged.
- Wheel-clamping fee: $75. Release within 1 hour of payment.
- Towing fee (third-party): $150 plus storage charges.
- Repeat offenders may be banned from the facility.

## Liability

CityPark is not liable for theft of items left visible inside vehicles. Use the in-facility lockers (available in Zone A, CityPark Central) for valuables. Damage caused by other customers should be reported to staff immediately.
