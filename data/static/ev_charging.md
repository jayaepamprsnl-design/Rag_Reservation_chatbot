# CityPark — EV Charging

## Electric Vehicle Charging at CityPark

CityPark supports the transition to zero-emission transport. EV charging bays are available at all three facilities, with capacity expanding quarterly.

## Charging Points by Facility

### CityPark Central (Indoor, Level 1 and Level 2)
- **12 bays** fitted with **Type 2 AC chargers** (22 kW) — suitable for all modern EVs.
- **4 bays** fitted with **CCS DC fast chargers** (50 kW) — available on Level 2 for shorter stays.
- Charging bays are covered and well-lit. Cable management columns provided.

### CityPark Eastside
- **6 bays** with **Type 2 AC chargers** (7 kW) located in the north corner of the outdoor lot.
- Suitable for stays of 2+ hours. Best for top-up charging during business hours.

### CityPark Airport
- **18 bays** with **Type 2 AC chargers** (22 kW) — ideal for long-stay airport customers who want to arrive home with a full charge.
- **6 bays** with **CCS DC fast chargers** (50 kW) near the shuttle bus stop for short-stay travelers.

## Compatible Vehicles and Connectors

| Connector   | Standard        | Compatible Vehicles                      |
|-------------|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Type 2 AC   | IEC 62196-2     | Tesla (with adapter), all European EVs   |
| CCS Combo 2 | IEC 62196-3     | Audi, BMW, Hyundai, Kia, VW, etc.        |

Tesla vehicles require a **CCS adapter** (sold separately or sometimes provided by Tesla). CHAdeMO adapters are not available on-site; please bring your own if needed.

## How to Use

1. **Book in advance.** EV bays are a separate reservation category — select "EV Charging Bay" when booking. Walk-in access is subject to availability but not guaranteed.
2. Pull into an EV bay and connect your cable to the charger column.
3. **Activate charging** via the CityPark app (tap "Start Charging" and scan the QR code on the column) or by tapping your contactless card on the column reader.
4. Charging begins within 30 seconds. Monitor status via the app or the LED indicator on the column (green = charging, amber = waiting, red = fault).
5. Charging stops automatically when your session ends or your vehicle is full.

## Pricing

- **AC charging (Type 2):** $0.35 per kWh.
- **DC fast charging (CCS):** $0.55 per kWh.
- Standard parking fee applies in addition to energy costs.
- Pre-paid account holders receive a **10% discount** on all charging sessions.

## Etiquette

- Move your vehicle once fully charged to free the bay for other EV drivers.
- Do not unplug another customer's vehicle.
- EV bays are strictly for charging vehicles only — ICE vehicles parked in EV bays will be issued a $50 penalty notice.
